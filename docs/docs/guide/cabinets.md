# Cabinets and shelves

Once the floor fills up, trophies go on the walls. **Trophy cabinets** and **trophy shelves** come in every wood: oak,
spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, pale oak, bamboo, crimson and warped.

| | Cabinet | Shelf |
|---|---|---|
| Size | two blocks wide, half a block deep | a board half a block deep on two arched brackets |
| Holds | four trophies | two trophies |
| Placed | anywhere | on the side of a solid block, and falls when that block goes |
| Joins | cabinets stacked on one another | shelves side by side |
| Crafted from | planks, gold ingots and a slab of its wood | three slabs and two gold nuggets, for two |

## Putting trophies on them

Use a trophy on a spot to put it there, and an empty hand to take it back. A trophy on a cabinet or shelf is drawn as
it is on its own pedestal, shrunk to fit: its mob or icon, its rank's light, flourishes and crown (no beam inside a
cabinet), and its text frame floating over it in front of the display. Breaking a cabinet or shelf drops it and its
trophies.

## Stacking and joining

Cabinets stacked on each other join, across woods too: they share one plank divider, with the gold running on through.
Shelves side by side join into one long shelf, with corner caps only at the ends.

## Trims

Cabinets and shelves come plain, with no trim. Use any block on one to trim it with that block: its rails, posts,
corner caps and brackets are drawn in that block's textures. A gold ingot (or a gold block) gives a gold trim, and an
axe takes the trim off again. A cabinet, once trimmed, is framed with a rope pattern and corner caps.

The trim is kept when it's broken, and a trimmed one's item shows its trim. (For packs: it's the
`trophypedestals:trim_material` component, `minecraft:gold_block` for gold.)

## Each trophy's own settings

Sneak and use an empty hand on a trophy on a cabinet or shelf to open its [adjust screen](adjust.md), with the settings
that suit a display: yaw, pitch and roll, text, pose and animation, spin, flourishes, sounds, light, crown and redstone. Its size
and place stay fixed. The settings belong to the spot: a trophy taken off keeps its own.
