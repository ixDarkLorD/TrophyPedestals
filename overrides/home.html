A Minecraft mod for NeoForge, Fabric and Forge

A pedestal for everything you've done.

Put any item on display in a soft cone of light. Keep the mobs you've slain, posed and animated, just as they looked, and the players you've beaten as statues. Every trophy shines in one of five ranks, and cabinets and wall shelves in every wood hold the collection.

How it works

Craft it, earn it, show it off.

A pedestal is a block you craft. A trophy is a pedestal that remembers something: what it was, who earned it, and when.

  1. ICraft a pedestal

    Six of a stone or wood around a gold ingot: 37 of vanilla's, or any block through data.

  2. IIEarn trophies

    Slain mobs and defeated players drop them now and then, and milestones and other mods give their own.

  3. IIIShow them off

    Size, pose and light each one, and fill cabinets and shelves with the rest.

The adjust screen

Every pedestal, just so.

Sneak and use one with an empty hand. Show it from half to twenty times its size, raise it up to ten blocks, turn it, set its text, freeze its mob or play an animation found from the mob itself, and turn its flourishes, sounds, light and crown on or off. It can even answer redstone.

More features

There's more in the hall.

Cases, mannequins, leaderboards and trophies you earn: here's how each one works.

  1. 🪟Glass cases

    Use glass, or any stained glass, on a pedestal to close a case over what it shows: a trophy, an item, a mannequin or a leaderboard.

  2. 🧍Mannequins

    Use an armor stand on an empty display pedestal, dress it in armor and give it something to hold. Pose it from five presets or limb by limb, save your own poses, or show it in your own skin.

  3. 🥇Leaderboards

    Use a Leaderboard Crest (a gold ingot and paper) on an empty display pedestal and pick a statistic: mob kills, distance walked, time played and more. Whoever leads it on the server stands there as their statue, and it changes the moment someone overtakes them.

  4. ✍️Engraving

    Rename a trophy in an anvil and its name is engraved on it.

  5. 📖The Mob Trophies Ledger

    A book and a gold nugget. It lists every mob, showing the ones you've had a trophy of and the rest as silhouettes, and sells you a new trophy of any you have collected for 5 levels.

  6. ✨Gilded trophies

    One mob trophy in fifty drops gilded, its mob standing on a gold pedestal.

  7. 🗄️Sets

    Fill a cabinet with four different mobs from one set (the Nether, the End, the Ocean, the Bosses or the Illagers) and it shows the set's name in a gold frame.

  8. 🎖️Earned trophies

    The first player to slay the Ender Dragon or the Wither gets a legendary trophy of it (the Warden and the Elder Guardian give epic ones), and milestones give their own: walking 100 km, slaying 1,000 mobs, catching 100 fish and playing 100 hours. Servers can also give one for each advancement.

Works with

Plays well with your pack.

Each one turns on by itself when its mod is installed. None of them is required.

  • JEIEvery mob trophy listed, one per mob, mods' mobs included.
  • REIThe same mob trophy pages, with the chance and rank of each.
  • JadeWhat a looked-at pedestal holds: its trophy, rank and who earned it.
  • The One ProbeThe same, for packs that use The One Probe.
  • FTBFTB QuestsA trophy reward, titled and ranked as the pack maker picks.
  • KubeJSRead, set and lock any pedestal's settings from server scripts (NeoForge and Forge).
  • { }Packs and modsLoot functions, a command, and a Java API for your own kinds of trophy and ranks.
