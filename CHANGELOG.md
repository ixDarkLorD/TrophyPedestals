# Changelog
This file is for listing all the changes to this project

## v0.1.0 Release | Unreleased
### ✨ New Features
- A trophy pedestal shown in a scene of its own (`PedestalBlockEntity.showTrophy`, as Tierwise's cinematic does) draws its full light with it in that scene: the lens glow, outlines, showcase cone, beam, halos, crown and flares.
- Split out of Tierwise as a mod of its own: everything below came from Tierwise's pedestals, under the `trophypedestals` namespace (old `tierwise:` blocks, items and data don't carry over). It runs alone; Tierwise, when installed, gives a trophy for every tier unlocked.
- Trophy kinds: a trophy records an achievement of a kind another mod registers (`TrophyKinds.register`, with its name, icon and default rank), and its subject; Tierwise's are `tierwise:tier`. A trophy whose kind's mod is gone keeps its record and shows its subject's id. `PedestalTeams` lets a mod supply the teams a pedestal's Team access uses (scoreboard teams by default).
- `/trophypedestals mob_trophy <players> <entity> [pedestal] [rarity]`, a server config (`config/trophypedestals-server`) and a `TrophyPedestals` KubeJS binding (NeoForge).
- Trophy cabinets and trophy shelves, in every wood. A cabinet is a shelf two blocks wide and half a block deep, framed (once trimmed) with a rope pattern and corner caps, that holds four trophies; a shelf hangs on a wall (only on the side of a solid block, and falls when that block goes): a board half a block deep on two arched brackets, with a gold lip and rim, holding two. Use a trophy on a spot to put it there and an empty hand to take it back. A cabinet is crafted from planks, gold ingots and a slab of its wood; two shelves from three slabs and two gold nuggets. Breaking one drops it and its trophies.
  - Cabinets stacked on each other, and shelves side by side, join (across woods too): stacked cabinets share one plank divider with the gold running on through, and joined shelves make one long shelf, with corner caps only at the ends.
  - Cabinets and shelves come plain, with no trim. Use any block on one to trim it with that block (drawn in its textures), or a gold ingot (or gold block) for gold; an axe takes the trim off again. The trim is kept when it's broken (the `trophypedestals:trim_material` component, `minecraft:gold_block` for gold), and a trimmed one's item shows its trim.
  - Trophies on them are drawn as on their own pedestals, shrunk to fit: their mob or icon, their rank's light, flourishes and crown (no beam inside a cabinet), and their text frame, floating over them in front of the display.
  - Sneak and use an empty hand on a trophy to open its adjust screen with the settings that suit a display (facing, text, pose and animation, spin, flourishes, sounds, light, crown, redstone); its size and place stay fixed. The settings are the spot's: a trophy taken off keeps its own.
- Creative tabs: "Trophy Pedestals" (every pedestal, cabinet and shelf), first, and "Trophy Pedestals: Mob Trophies" after it (a trophy of every living mob in the game, mods' included, ranked by toughness and sorted by rank, then name). The mob trophies are listed in JEI too, one per mob.
- Trophy items in inventory slots are drawn once and kept, so a screen full of them (the mob trophies tab, JEI) costs no more than any other items; their mobs are sized to fit inside the slot.
- The pedestal screen keeps one height on every tab; a tab with more than fits scrolls, by the wheel or by its scroll bar (dragged, or clicked to jump).
- A mob or statue on a trophy pedestal can play an animation on loop, picked from a list found from the mob itself (each one tried on its model, so only those that move it are listed): walking, running and attacking, its own animations (a warden's roar, a camel sitting down), its own states (a fox sitting or sleeping, a zombie's raised arms) and its own poses (a parrot's). A pedestal's text can be shown from half to three times its size, frame and all. A trophy's adjustment screen is in tabs, each a single column: Display (size, height, facing), Text (when it shows, its height, its size), Behavior (spinning, flourishes, sounds, a legendary trophy's crown, redstone) and, for a mob or statue, Pose (its pose and animation); the last tab used opens next time. `PedestalSettings` has both new settings too.
- Recipes for six custom pedestals, crafted like the others: amethyst block, obsidian, copper block, mossy stone bricks, smooth stone and polished blackstone bricks.
- A trophy pedestal's light (its glows, outlines, beam, halos, crown shards and flares) is drawn after the rest of the world, so clouds no longer paint over it.
- A pedestal's showcase light (its lit lens, the cone of light out of the well and its frame) can be turned off from its adjustment screen, on a trophy (Behavior tab) or a display pedestal, and through `PedestalSettings` and KubeJS's `light`.
- The pedestal adjustment screen explains its options: a ? after each section's name, and each Behavior button, shows a tooltip while hovered.
- Trophy pedestals: a trophy (Tierwise gives one for every tier unlocked) is a pedestal that, placed, shows its icon turning and glowing above it, with a spiral of light in the tier's color rising out of it, and the tier's name, who unlocked it (player, team or the whole server) and when floating over it, the text and the light showing while you look at it within reach. Breaking it keeps its record. A tier's `"reveal": {"trophy": ...}` picks the pedestal (`"trophypedestals:quartz_pedestal"`) or turns it off (`false`). Pedestals come in vanilla's stones and woods (37 of them, each crafted from six of its material around a gold ingot, a stone one mined with a pickaxe and a wooden one with an axe), each carved with a gold diamond on its column, its column fluted, and a dark glass lens raised in a gold bezel in its well that fades up to its trophy's rank color as it turns on and back down as it turns off. Any other block, a mod's included, makes a custom pedestal (`trophypedestals:custom_pedestal` with the `trophypedestals:pedestal_material` component), drawn with that block's own textures and named after it, which data packs give out with recipes and loot tables (the example pack has an amethyst one). A crafted pedestal displays any item put on it as a showcase, turning slowly in a soft cone of warm light. Data packs make trophies with the `trophypedestals:set_trophy` loot function (`kind` and `subject`), and mods with `TrophyPedestals` (`net.ixdarklord.trophypedestals.api`), for any kind of achievement a mod registers with `TrophyKinds`.
- Trophy ranks: every trophy has a rarity, from common to uncommon, rare, epic and legendary, each with its own colors, looks and sounds, growing from a grey glow with a faint flare to a golden beam of light with shimmering outlines, a pulsing double flare, a crown of light shards, circling sparks, bursts of light, a fanfare when placed and a beacon's hum. A tier picks its trophies' with `"reveal": {"trophy_rarity": ...}`; the loot function (`"rarity"`), `/tierwise trophy ... [rarity]` and `TrophyPedestals.create` can name another. Mods add ranks with `PedestalRarities.register` and extra effects with `PedestalEffects`. The rank shows over the trophy and in its tooltip. Trophy items are named after their tier ("Diamond Age Trophy") in their rank's color, keep the name when placed and broken, and show the tier's icon floating over the pedestal in the inventory and in hand. Distant trophies draw less, and an empty pedestal's well shows a dark glass lens set into its material, which lights up in the rank's color once it holds something. A trophy's text sits on a panel in its rank's own frame (from a plain grey line to a bevelled gold frame with faceted corners and a winged crest), drawn over the pedestal's light.
- Mob trophies: a mob a player kills sometimes drops a pedestal showing it turning over the pedestal, with its name, who slew it and when; the ender dragon and wither give legendary ones, the warden and elder guardian epic ones (entity type tags `trophypedestals:legendary_mob_trophies` and `trophypedestals:epic_mob_trophies`). Other mods' mobs drop them too (any living thing but players and armor stands; opt one out with the `trophypedestals:no_mob_trophy` tag). Packs hand out mob trophies with the `trophypedestals:set_mob_trophy` loot function (as quest rewards, or in a mob's own loot table for a sure drop, keeping its look) and `/trophypedestals mob_trophy <players> <entity> [pedestal] [rarity]`; mods with `TrophyPedestals.createMob`. With the server's `player_trophies` on (off by default), a player killed by another player can drop a trophy of their statue in their own skin. A tier's trophy unlocked by a player can show that player's statue in place of the tier's icon (Display: Statue in its adjustment screen), standing, crouching, swimming, gliding or sleeping. A trophy keeps how its mob looked: its variant and colors, whether it was a baby, its gear and saddle, and the name it was given, which the trophy then goes by (mods' mobs' own looks included). The server config turns them on or off (`mob_trophies`), sets the chance (`mob_trophy_chance`, 1% by default) and their rank (`mob_trophy_rarity`: none, shown as a display pedestal is, by default; by the mob's strength; or by how many of that mob the player has killed, with the counts for each rank set by `mob_trophy_kills_uncommon`, `_rare`, `_epic` and `_legendary`, and other counts for some mobs or entity tags in `mob_trophy_kills_overrides`, where bosses need fewer by default). The cinematic reveal shows the tier's trophy on its altar, on the tier's own trophy pedestal and turning the same item as the trophy it gives (its `block`, which the tier's trophies then show too, or the tier's icon), and takes its colors from the trophy's rank unless the tier sets its own.
- Sneaking and using a pedestal with an empty hand opens an adjustment screen: when its text shows (on look, always, or hidden), its display size (0.5x to 20x, with its effects growing to match), its display height (half a block down to ten up), its facing, spinning or still, a mob trophy's mob moving or holding one pose (Mob Pose), the animation a mob or statue plays on loop (picked from a list of what it can play: idle, walk, run, attack and the mob's own), flourishes on or off (off leaves only the showcase light and its outlines), sounds on or off, its text's height, and redstone (ignore, show while powered, or hide while powered); each slider has its own reset button, and Reset puts everything back. The legendary text frame wears a crown, with its winged crest hanging below. A lit pedestal's light grows with its trophy's rank, from a display's 10 to a legendary trophy's full 15. A legendary trophy also wears a gold crown floating over what it shows, turning with it. Mods can force a pedestal's settings and lock them against every player with `PedestalSettings` (`net.ixdarklord.trophypedestals.api`), refusing a player who tries with their own message or silently. The server config's `pedestal_access` decides who may adjust it: its owner (and operators) or anyone.
